Productivity planner

Most productivity tools keep adding features until they need a manual. Pomop stays small: you add tasks, it picks one, you focus on it, and then it picks the next.

Pomop is a digital planner: a free web app you can install on your phone or computer, not a printed planner or a PDF template. There's no account to create; your tasks are saved in your browser.

The loop

Every part of Pomop exists to support one cycle: capture a task, see it in context on the calendar, get a decision about what to work on, focus on it, mark it complete, and see it in your history. Then it starts again with the next task. Each step lives on a page you can reach from the menu (Home, Tasks, Calendar, History), so there's no separate mode to learn.

What's deliberately left out

No projects, no subtasks, no kanban boards, no team chat, no AI assistant, no habit tracker. They're reasonable features for a different kind of tool. Pomop leaves them out so the main screen stays about one thing, the recommendation.

The pieces

  • Tasks — capture and filter by Today, Upcoming, Overdue, or Completed
  • Calendar — see due dates at a glance, add or reschedule with a click
  • Decision Engine — one recommendation, with a reason, every time
  • Focus — a distraction-free timer for the recommended task, one click away
  • History — a plain log of what got done, no gamification

Frequently asked questions

Is this a replacement for Notion or Trello?

No, and it's not trying to be. Pomop doesn't do projects, subtasks, kanban boards, or team collaboration. Its job is deciding what you should work on next, and the other features (tasks, calendar, focus, history) are only as big as that job needs.

Why doesn’t it have streaks or points?

Streaks reward opening the app every day, whether or not you worked on the right thing. Pomop's History page shows what got done and how long it took, and that's all.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no feature paywall. Pomop.app is free to use, and never sells or transmits your task data.
