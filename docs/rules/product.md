# Product rules

Established from market research on 2026-11-01; commercial demand is unproven.
- Serve people who have free time but stall choosing or starting an activity.
- Prioritize their own interests; offer optional starter ideas when they have none.
- Offer one feasible action and a concrete first step; allow skip, manual choice, rest, and exit.
- Keep setup optional and brief. Do not add a feed, social layer, or streak obligations to the core journey.
- Distinguish shipped behavior from proposals. Single-item suggestions and privacy are not unique advantages.
- Validate repeat use before charging; treat continuation, curated packs, and sync as pricing hypotheses.
